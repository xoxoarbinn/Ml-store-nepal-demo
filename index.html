How to Buy

Your step-by-step guide to purchasing a Mobile Legends account safely.

1

Browse the Marketplace

Visit our Marketplace page and use the filters to find accounts that match your desired rank, skin count, and budget. Each listing shows detailed info including rank, skins, points, and highlighted rare skins.

2

Contact the Seller

Click the "Buy Now" button on any listing to open a WhatsApp chat with the seller. Discuss account details, ask for screenshots or video proof, and negotiate if needed.

3

Use Escrow for Payment

Never send money directly. Use our escrow service or a trusted middleman. The seller provides account credentials to the escrow, and you verify the account before releasing payment.

4

Verify & Secure the Account

Once you receive the credentials, log in immediately and change the password, bind email, and enable 2FA. Check that all promised skins and ranks are present before confirming the deal.

💡 Pro Tip

Always ask for a screen recording of the account lobby and inventory before making any payment. If a deal seems too good to be true, it probably is. Stick to verified sellers and use escrow whenever possible.

Our Safety Process

We prioritize your security with multiple layers of protection.

Guaranteed Escrow

All transactions are protected by our escrow system. Your payment is held securely until you verify and confirm receipt of the account. Sellers only get paid after buyers are satisfied.

Verified Sellers

Every seller on our platform goes through a verification process. We check their identity, account ownership proof, and transaction history to ensure you're dealing with legitimate traders.

Anti-Fraud Checks

Our system monitors listings for suspicious activity. We verify account screenshots, cross-check prices, and flag any potentially fraudulent accounts before they reach buyers.

Account Verification

We require sellers to provide proof of account ownership including lobby screenshots, skin inventory, and match history before their listing goes live.

Direct WhatsApp Communication

All buyer-seller communication happens through WhatsApp for transparency. This creates a clear record and allows for real-time negotiation and verification.

Scam Prevention Tips

Never share your payment details outside escrow. Always verify account credentials before releasing funds. Report suspicious sellers immediately to our support team.
